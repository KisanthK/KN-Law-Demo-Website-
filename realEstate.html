Ontario Real Estate Law

Buying and selling homes or land in Ontario may appear simple, but the underlying legal risks are significant.

Closing without rigorous oversight risks missing critical banking and registry deadlines—technicalities that can cause a transaction to fail. KN LAW provides the precision-focused advocacy necessary to protect your investment and ensure a seamless closing.

01

Bespoke Agreements

Drafting ironclad Purchase & Sale agreements tailored specifically to your transaction requirements and unique property conditions.

02

Legal Execution

Formal signing and witnessing of all solicitor documents, ensuring strict adherence to Law Society of Ontario compliance standards.

03

Title Verification

Exhaustive searches of land titles to identify legal property owners, uncover hidden liens, and resolve encumbrances before closing.

04

Title Transfer

Managing formal property transitions through the Ontario Land Registry system with meticulous accuracy to secure your ownership.

Mortgage Discharge

Securing the legal release of existing loans and ensuring all financial obligations are cleared and verified upon the completion of a sale

06

Trust Management

The secure holding of real estate deposits within our regulated solicitor trust accounts to ensure fund integrity throughout the deal.

Deadline Compliance

Proactive monitoring of banking, realtor, and legal milestones to prevent technical defaults on your Offer to Purchase.
