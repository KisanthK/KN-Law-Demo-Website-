Our Practice Area.

LSO Compliant Advocacy

Providing sophisticated legal counsel for the Ontario and New Brunswick real estate landscapes.

01 / Transactional Excellence
01

Buying Real Estate

We manage the extensive legal formalities involved in acquisitions to ensure your legal rights are secured. This includes title transfers, Agreement of Sales or Purchase reviews, and coordination of mortgage refinancing.

02

Selling Real Estate

Selling property requires precise legal completion to ensure a valid transaction. KN LAW handles all formalities to provide a smooth closing process and total peace of mind.

03

Transfer of Title

Execution of the most crucial stage of the transaction: the formal transfer of property ownership from existing owners to new owners via the land registry.

04

Survivorship Applications

In cases where property is co-owned by joint-tenants and one passes away, we submit formal applications to update title records and remove the name of the deceased owner.

02 / Financial Specialization
05

Mortgage Refinance

We facilitate the superseding of old mortgages with new agreements, ensuring the remaining balance of previous mortgages is properly discharged and paid off.

06

Private Mortgage Financing

Representation for non-institutional lending. Per the Financial and Consumer Services Commission (New Brunswick), we facilitate loans from individuals, including family, co-workers, or private investors.

07

Reverse Mortgage Registrations

Specialized for individuals 55+, allowing home equity access without selling. We manage the registration of these loans, which require no regular payments but carry unique interest structures.

Independent Legal Representation
